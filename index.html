What is Honk?

Honk.dk was built for the rebellious of us who think abbreviations have gone too far. You know the feeling — someone drops a TLA, an OKR, a RACI, or a GTM in the middle of a sentence and half the room quietly nods along while understanding nothing. So when it happens, don't just sit there. Honk.

A honk is a friendly but firm reminder: say the full word. Not the code. Not the shortcut. The actual thing you mean. Because when everyone has to decode before they can think, the conversation is already lost.

I just got honkled at?

When you hear a honk, don't be offended — be grateful. Someone cared enough to ask. Open up to the honks, spell it out, and watch the room actually follow along.
